# Premiere Herbarium marketing and sales plan

**Owner.** Chance owns the public release and approves campaign copy.

**Audience.** Art collectors and premiere viewers. **Offer.** Let visitors examine the twenty-five premiere designs while claims are paused.

| Proposed timing | Public material | Desired outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–3 | Reveal one paired detail from two edition variants with their design numbers. | Give the audience one verifiable reason to try the project. |
| Days 4–10 | Share the reviewed demo or sample through the project page and one relevant creator or community channel. | Browse the exhibit. |
| Days 11–28 | Publish a build note showing what the trial revealed and one change made from feedback. | 10 visitors inspect at least two pieces. |

### Sales test

Let visitors request print information by design number. Consider a paid proof print once three distinct people request the same design and reproduction rights are confirmed. Test demand for an artist-proof print using separate interest capture. Count paid prints only against fulfilled orders.

### Measurement and decision

Use `premiere-herbarium-first-public-build` for the pilot. Record a 28-day prelaunch baseline where available, then observe each participant for seven days after their first visit. Current audience, conversion and net revenue baselines are unknown. The numbers above are proposed targets. Distinguish visits, qualified actions, buyer inquiries and paid receipts. Keep private identifiers in the authorized reporting system. If the outcome cannot be attributed, label it unknown.

Proposed pilot budget is $0 for distribution and two founder hours for the first demonstration. At day 7, inspect the biggest point of friction. At day 28, expand only if the target action was measured and the sales test produced identifiable demand. Report fulfilled sales net of refunds and direct costs. A product serving trust or internal operations may have no direct sales at all.

**Prerequisite for external promotion.** Prepared artwork and metadata do not establish issuance or a wallet receipt. Issuance is outside this exhibit. Draft copy and placements go through the existing approval process.
