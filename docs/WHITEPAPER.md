# Premiere Herbarium
## Whitepaper · public developer edition

**November 25, 2026 · Art collection and metadata exhibit**

### Goal and audience

Let visitors examine the twenty-five premiere designs while claims are paused. The immediate audience is art collectors and premiere viewers.

### Design and implementation

The public exhibit indexes numbered art and collection metadata. It reads static files and has no wallet or signing dependency. Use stable artwork numbers across media, captions and any future collectible metadata. The standalone gallery avoids making a chain operation part of browsing.

### What a contributor can establish

The public entry page returned HTTP 200 and rendered local content in Chromium at a 390 × 844 viewport. Artwork inspection, schematic rarity, a prepared claim and a confirmed mint represent different events. This repository can substantiate the artwork only.

### Evidence and outstanding work

Prepared artwork and metadata do not establish issuance or a wallet receipt. Issuance is outside this exhibit. The next acceptance case is concrete. Open any numbered design and confirm that its displayed number matches its asset and can be shared.

### Place in POTIFY

Test demand for an artist-proof print using separate interest capture. Count paid prints only against fulfilled orders. The marketing plan gives the test window and the evidence for a commercial outcome.
